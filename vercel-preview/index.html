How does a hypnosis appointment work?+

Your appointment begins with a 90-minute whole-life analysis. Hypnosis follows, with a personal recording made on your phone. Jody asks you to listen at bedtime for at least 21 nights. The analysis is one part of the appointment; total appointment time is confirmed with Jody.

What does it cost?+

A standard hypnosis session is $250 AUD. Quit smoking costs more. Enquire about pricing for quit vaping, mental gastric bypass, monthly life coaching, equestrian mindset, speaking and commentating.

Is a request a confirmed booking?+

Submitting this form sends a session request. Jody will confirm the appointment with you. Nothing is payable upfront; cancellations within 24 hours incur half the session fee.

Are the book, podcast and audios available?+

Live Sexy Now is coming soon, the podcast is in production and the free audios are being recorded. Join the book or audio list to register your interest.

Hypnotherapy and coaching can sit alongside medical or psychological care. They do not replace it, and individual experiences differ. Please keep personal medical details for your appointment.
